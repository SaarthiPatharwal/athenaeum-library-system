How it works

Every title gets a call number the moment it's added. Issuing a copy quietly reduces what's left on the shelf; returning one brings it back. When a title reaches zero, its status flips to out on loan and the Issue button steps aside until a copy comes home. This page keeps everything in memory for the session — reload the page and the shelf resets to its starting collection.
